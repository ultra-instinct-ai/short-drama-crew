# Compliance — AI disclosure, truthful depiction, testimonials

What an AI-made ad must disclose, where, and what it must not claim, as of Sep 2026. Platform
policies change and laws phase in; rows marked *(verify)* were inconsistent between sources
and should be checked against the primary text before a campaign relies on them. This is a
working checklist, not legal advice; the client's counsel signs off on the final ad.

## AI-content disclosure by platform

| Platform | What triggers disclosure | How it is done | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | realistic altered or synthetic content: a real-looking person saying or doing something they did not, a real event or place altered, a realistic scene that did not happen | tick "altered or synthetic content" at upload; YouTube shows a label (more prominent on sensitive topics) | not needed for clearly unreal / animated / stylised content, beauty filters or a script; YouTube may auto-label; repeated non-disclosure is penalised |
| Meta (Facebook / Instagram) | ads made or edited with Meta's own generative tools get an "AI info" label automatically (Feb 2025) | for other AI content: the "AI info" self-label; political / social-issue ads must disclose photorealistic AI people, events or audio | third-party AI-detection labelling from 1 Jun 2026 |
| TikTok Ads Manager | realistic AI visuals, cloned voices, synthetic people, photoreal AI product renders | the "contains AI-generated content" toggle on the ad; organic posts use the AI-generated content label | C2PA content credentials auto-label AI uploads since Jan 2025 |
| Google Ads (display / search) | election ads with synthetic people or events must carry a disclosure *(verify scope)*; other ads have no AI label and fall under the misrepresentation policy | the election-ad disclosure setting | |
| 抖音 / 快手 / 小红书 / 视频号 / B站 / 微博 | any AI-generated or synthesised content | the platform's 「AI生成」 self-declaration at upload, plus the visible mark below | required by the 标识办法 |

Practice: when in doubt, label. A label costs nothing; a removed ad and a penalised account
cost the campaign.

## Jurisdictions

| Jurisdiction | Rule | In force | What it requires of an ad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| China (mainland) | 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》 (Measures for Labelling AI-Generated and Synthetic Content) | 2025-09-01 | **explicit** label: a visible 「AI生成」 / 「人工智能生成」 mark on images and video (start, end or throughout, legible), and audible or text notice on audio; **implicit** label: metadata / watermark carrying producer and content identifiers; platforms must check and add their own; removing or hiding a label is prohibited |
| European Union | AI Act Article 50 (transparency obligations) | 2026-08-02 *(verify: the "digital omnibus" may shift dates; existing systems referenced to 2026-12-02)* | deepfakes (AI images, audio or video resembling real people, places, events) must be disclosed as artificially generated or manipulated; generative outputs must carry machine-readable marking (metadata / watermark); a clearly artistic or satirical piece needs only a non-intrusive disclosure |
| United States | FTC Act §5 and "Operation AI Comply" (2024–) | ongoing | no AI-specific label mandate, but AI does not soften the deception rules below; several states have deepfake-in-advertising and political-ad laws |
| United Kingdom | ASA / CAP Code (practice reading, not researched for this file) | ongoing | ads must not mislead by omission; AI imagery of a product must show the product as sold |
| Hong Kong / Taiwan / Singapore | general fair-trading and advertising law; platform rules apply (practice reading, not researched for this file) | ongoing | follow the platform label; disclose synthetic people |

## Misleading depiction (FTC standard, applied globally as practice)

- **Show the product as it is.** An AI packshot that thickens the cream, brightens the juice or
  enlarges the screen is a misrepresentation. The packshot reference exists to prevent this.
- **No fabricated results.** No before / after the product cannot deliver, no AI-invented "test
  results", no synthetic "lab" imagery presented as evidence.
- **No synthetic testimonials without disclosure.** An AI-generated person reading a review is
  not a customer; it may only appear as an obviously fictional presenter, and never with a claim
  like "real customer".
- **Demonstrations must be real.** A demo clip generated by AI must depict something the product
  does; the AI is the camera, not the proof.
- **Claims need substantiation** before the ad is made: "clinically proven", "#1", "fastest" each
  need a file the client holds.
- **Comparisons** name no competitor's real mark unless the client accepts comparative-advertising
  law in every market the ad reaches.

## Testimonial and endorsement disclosure

| Case | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Real customer, real words | keep the record (consent, the original review); typical results or a "results may vary" line where the result is not typical |
| Real person, AI-generated voice or likeness | written consent for the synthetic use; label as AI on every platform that asks; never imply live footage |
| Paid creator or influencer | a clear, visible #ad / #sponsored / 「廣告」 / 「合作」 disclosure at the start, not buried in tags |
| Fictional presenter (AI person) | the presenter makes no first-person claims of having used the product as a customer; AI disclosure on |
| Expert or professional | the person must hold the qualification; AI cannot dress a stand-in as a doctor |
| Voice clone | explicit consent from the voice's owner, held on file; disclose where the platform asks |

## Pre-flight checklist

1. Product depicted exactly as sold (label, size, colour, contents) against the packshot.
2. Every claim on screen or in voice-over has a substantiation file.
3. No real person, voice or mark used without written consent; no competitor mark.
4. AI disclosure set on every platform that asks, and the visible 「AI生成」 mark plus metadata
   for any mainland-China placement; EU deepfake disclosure where a real-looking person appears.
5. Testimonials are real and on file, or the presenter is clearly fictional and labelled.
6. Legal lines, small print and prices are overlays of approved text, never generated.
