# Action style `superhero` — 超级英雄

**The fight:** spectacle and character at once. Powers switch on with visible energy; heroes are
framed **from low angles** to look mythic; the three-point landing, the power reveal in slow
motion, the team shot in a line; destruction on a city scale in the background; and a beat of
banter between blows. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `none` with a bright blockbuster daylight, or `low-key` for a night battle. Costumes
with distinct colours per hero; the power has its own colour too.

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are medium**: 2.5–4.5 s.
- **Sizes**: extreme wide for scale (the hero small against a collapsing tower), wide for the
  landing and the team shot, close for the power igniting in the eyes or the hands.
- **Camera**: **low `height`** (low or ground) for every hero moment, `Pedestal Up` rising with a
  hero into frame, `Arc Shot` around the team, `Push In` on the power reveal. `Shake Slightly` on
  a big impact.
- **Speed**: slow motion for the power reveal and the landing; real time for the fight.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the landing** | extreme wide: a streak of light falling toward the street (establish) → wide, low angle: she lands in a three-point landing, the asphalt cracking in a ring (leap, impact) → wide: cars lift and settle around her (consequence) → close, low angle: she looks up (performance) |
| **the power reveal** | close: his hands, energy crackling to life (power, slow motion) → medium, low angle: the glow reaches his eyes (power) → wide: a blast of light hurls the villain across the plaza (strike, impact) → wide: the villain crashes into a fountain (consequence) |
| **the team shot** | wide, low angle, Arc Shot: the heroes stand in a line as the camera circles, each power glowing (stance) |
| **the city scale** | extreme wide: two figures clash above the skyline, a building collapsing behind (strike, impact) → close: a civilian looking up (reaction) |

## Staging

City plazas, rooftops, bridges, skies, collapsing towers, a burning street. Always a sense of
scale: the destruction is in the background, the character in the foreground.

## Sound

Energy hums and crackles, the boom of a landing, concrete cracking, the swell of the heroic
score. Music is loud here: `enter` with the hero, `swell` on the reveal, `drop` for the banter.

## Example `motion.action`

- `she lands in a three-point landing and the asphalt cracks in a ring around her`
- `energy crackles to life around his hands, in slow motion`
- `a blast of light hurls the villain across the plaza`

## Review questions

- Is the hero shot from below at their big moment?
- Is the power visible — its colour, its glow — and revealed in slow motion?
- Is there a sense of scale: something huge happening behind a person?
