# Action style `street-brawl` — 写实街头格斗

**The fight:** ugly and desperate. Two or a few bodies in a space too small for them — a
corridor, a stairwell, a bathroom, an alley — slamming into walls and doors, grappling, falling,
getting up exhausted. No technique on display; survival. The camera is in there with them,
jostled. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `low-key` (a single bulb in a corridor, fluorescent tubes in a toilet, a sodium lamp in
an alley). Sweat, rain, grime.

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are short to medium**: 2–3.5 s.
- **Sizes**: medium and close, cramped; a wide down the corridor to open and to reset (the long
  corridor fight is the signature: one side of the frame to the other).
- **Camera**: handheld (`Shake Slightly`; `Shake Strongly` in the worst of it), close, at chest
  height. A `Truck Left` / `Truck Right` along a corridor for the one-take feeling.
- **Speed**: real time. Nothing stylised.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the corridor** | wide down the corridor: one man against six (establish) → wide, truck: he swings a hammer at the first (strike, impact) → medium: the man slams into the wall (reaction) → wide, truck: the crowd pushes him back along the corridor (grapple) |
| **the wall** | medium handheld: he drives the other man backwards into the door (grapple, impact) → close: the door frame splinters (consequence) |
| **the ground** | medium: both fall to the floor, grappling (grapple) → close: a hand reaching for a knife on the tiles (pressure) → close: the knife skids away under a kick (kick, impact) → medium: both lie gasping (consequence) |
| **after** | medium: he gets up slowly, one hand on the wall, bleeding from the brow (performance) |

## Staging and props

Corridors, stairwells, toilets, kitchens, alleys, cramped apartments, a car's back seat. Whatever
is there: a hammer, a bottle, a chair, a fire extinguisher, a door.

## Sound

Bodies on walls and doors, breaking glass, grunts, ragged breath, shoes on tiles, a phone
buzzing in the middle of it. Music: none or a low drone.

## Example `motion.action`

- `he slams the man backwards into the door`
- `both men fall to the tiles, grappling`
- `he gets up slowly with one hand on the wall`

## Review questions

- Is the space too small? Does the fight use its walls, doors and floor?
- Is the camera handheld and close?
- Does anyone look tired or hurt after?
