# Cinematography preset `apocalypse` — 末日 / 末世 / 废土 / 丧尸末日 / 末世囤货

The look: **the world after the fall.** Ruins of concrete and rust, overgrowth, dust and ash
hanging in the air; by day a harsh bleached sun through a broken roof, by night only what the
survivors keep burning — a fire in a barrel, a flare, a flashlight, a generator bulb, headlights —
with the dead city black behind them. It is **not** `scifi-soft` (no neon, no holograms: the city
is dead) and **not** `horror` (the world is seen; the danger is the people and the horde in it, the
hunger, the cold). A zombie apocalypse uses this look; its scares are still cut in the `horror`
grammar (`grammar-horror.md`, `threat.kind: horde`). The mechanics shared by every preset are in
`cinematography.md`.

`init` picks it from 末日 / 末世 / 废土 / 核战 / 天灾 / apocalypse / wasteland, zombies or not. A
末世重生囤货 爽剧 gets this look with `drama` cutting; add `--grammar horror` only when it is meant to
frighten.

## 1. Light — what still burns

| when | source | how to write it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| day | the harsh sun through a collapsed roof, a broken window, a cracked windscreen; dust in the shaft | `harsh bleached sunlight through the collapsed roof, dust hanging in the shaft, the rest of the mall in grey shadow` |
| night | a fire in a barrel, a campfire, a burning car | `lit only by the barrel fire, faces hard on one side, the street beyond black` |
| night, moving | a flashlight, a flare (red, sputtering), car headlights | `the red flare sputters in his hand, the corridor beyond swallowed by smoke` |
| inside the shelter | a generator bulb, a camping lantern, candles | `a single generator bulb over the table, swinging when the generator coughs` |

- **Refused sources** (gated): neon, holograms, street lights, billboards, shop windows, city
  lights, working screens, chandeliers — the lights of the city before the fall. A screen may *be*
  in the frame (dead, cracked), never a light.
- **Key** hard from the side, behind or above (the noon sun); never frontal (a clean studio
  portrait). At least half the cuts with people.
- **Palette**: ash grey, rust, concrete, sick yellow, dried-blood brown; one accent — the red flare,
  the yellow hazard tape, the green of overgrowth. Desaturated, never glossy.
- **Stop**: up to T4 — the wasteland is seen: daylight exteriors hold depth so the ruin reads.

## 2. Composition and camera

- **Scale**: a tiny figure on an empty highway, a crowd of stalled cars, a collapsed overpass — step
  back to show what fell. On 9:16, a medium-wide that keeps the ruin above or below the figure.
- **Texture**: grime on faces, layered scavenged clothes, makeshift weapons, barricades of
  furniture and car doors, spray-painted warnings on walls (text policy: exact, or blank).
- **Camera**: a handheld documentary urgency in danger and on the move; locked off for the quiet
  of the shelter. A horde is shot wide and high so it reads as one mass (`action-zombie-horde.md`).

## 3. Review questions

- Could each light still burn after the fall? Who keeps it running?
- Is anything glossy, clean or neon? Dirty it or kill it.
- Does at least one cut per scene show the scale of the ruin?
