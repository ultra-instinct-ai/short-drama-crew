# Action style `historical-realist` — 写实冷兵器

**The fight:** heavy, clumsy, desperate and final. Swords and spears have real weight; fighters
tire, slip in the mud, gasp inside armour; exchanges are short because a blade ends them. No
flying, no flourishes: the drama is the standoff before and the exhaustion after. Shared rules
and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `costume-dark` (firelight, smoke, earthy texture). Rain, mud and grey sky suit it.

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are medium**: 2.5–4 s. The standoff can hold longer; the clash itself is quick.
- **Sizes**: medium and close, inside the fight, with a wide to open (the geography, the scale of
  the battle) and to reset.
- **Camera**: handheld — `Shake Slightly` inside the fight, `Shake Strongly` in the chaos of a
  battle — close to the fighters, low. `Static Shot` for the standoff.
- **Speed**: real time. At most one slow cut: the aftermath, the fighter on his knees.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the standoff** | wide: two fighters facing across the mud, rain falling (establish) → close: his hand tightening on the hilt (stance) → close: her eyes (pressure) |
| **the clash** | medium handheld: he charges and swings (slash) → close: the blades bind, grinding (block, impact) → medium: she shoves him back into the mud (reaction) |
| **the killing blow** | medium: the spear thrusts in (stab, impact) → close: his face as it lands (reaction) → wide: he sinks to his knees in the rain (consequence) |
| **the battlefield** | extreme wide: two lines of soldiers in the smoke (establish) → medium handheld: the hero in the press, shields crashing (grapple) → close: a man falls at his feet (consequence) → wide: the hero alone in the smoke, breathing (performance) |
| **after** | medium: the victor leaning on the sword, breath steaming, blood on the blade (performance) |

## Staging and props

Muddy courtyards, rain, city walls, burning villages, forests, battlefields in smoke. Armour that
creaks, dented helmets, notched blades, torn cloth. Blood, if `params.violence: blood`, is real
and restrained: on the blade, on the hands, spattered on a face.

## Sound

Metal on metal, armour creaking, feet in mud, grunts and ragged breath, rain, the silence after.
Music: low drums or nothing; the style often plays better with no score.

## Example `motion.action`

- `he swings the heavy blade down at her shoulder`
- `she catches it on her own blade and the two edges grind together`
- `he sinks to his knees in the mud, the sword slipping from his hand`

## Review questions

- Does the weapon feel heavy? Does anyone tire?
- Is there a standoff before and a silence after?
- Is the camera close and handheld inside the fight?
