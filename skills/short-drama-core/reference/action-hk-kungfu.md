# Action style `hk-kungfu` — 港式功夫

**The fight:** crisp, fast, readable kung fu. Every strike and block is framed full-body so the
audience can follow the exchange; the hero uses **whatever is to hand** (a chair, a ladder, a
bench, a mop, a steamer basket); the key hit lands with a speed ramp and is **shown twice from
two angles**; and between blows there is physical comedy: the shaken hand after hitting a
pot, the look to camera. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `none` or `low-key`. The moves must read, so the light is clearer than a thriller's: a
market, a restaurant kitchen, a warehouse, a construction site, a tea house.

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are short**: 2–3 s (average 2–3 s; the 2 s minimum is the floor). Speed comes from the
  rhythm of short cuts and ramps, not from 1-second cuts the model cannot render cleanly.
- **Sizes**: a locked-off **wide or medium** for the exchange, full body, both fighters in frame;
  inserts on the prop and the hit.
- **Camera**: mostly `Static Shot` so the choreography reads; a quick `Pan Left` / `Pan Right`
  (whip-like) to follow a throw; `Push In` on the comic reaction.
- **Speed**: write the ramp into the cut (`in a speed ramp, the kick slows at the moment of
  contact and snaps back`). **The repeat**: the decisive hit is cut twice, the second from a
  new angle (the 3-hit repeat for the big one).

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the prop fight** | wide: he backs into a kitchen, cleavers and pans everywhere (establish) → medium: he snatches a pan from the stove (draw) → medium: he blocks a pole with the pan (block, impact) → close: the pan rings and he shakes his stinging hand (reaction, comedy) |
| **the repeat hit** | wide: the villain charges (establish) → medium: the hero's flying kick lands, speed ramp (kick, impact) → wide from the side: the same kick again, the villain flying back (consequence) → close: the villain's stunned face on the floor (reaction) |
| **the ladder** | wide: three thugs close in (establish) → medium: he swings the ladder round (strike, impact) → wide: the three go down in a row (consequence) → medium: he steps through the rungs, grinning (performance) |
| **the pose** | after the last blow, a medium on the hero settling into a stance, a beat of stillness (stance) — the 定格 |

## Staging and props

Markets, dim sum kitchens, bamboo scaffolding, fishing docks, warehouses, rooftops. Everyday
props as weapons are the style: chairs, benches, ladders, brooms, umbrellas, woks, bicycles.

## Sound

Exaggerated punch and kick sfx (whooshes and snaps), the clang of props, the crowd gasping, a
comic sting after a joke. Music: percussive, driving; `duck` for the comic beat.

## Example `motion.action`

- `he swings the wooden bench round into the thug's ribs`
- `she ducks under the pole` (a dodge is one move)
- `he shakes his stinging hand and blows on his knuckles` (the comic beat)

## Review questions

- Can you follow every exchange — both fighters in frame, full body?
- Is there a prop in the hero's hands at least once?
- Is the decisive hit shown twice, with a ramp?
- Is there one laugh inside the fight?
