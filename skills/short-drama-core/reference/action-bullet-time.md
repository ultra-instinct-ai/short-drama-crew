# Action style `bullet-time` — 子弹时间

**The fight:** time itself is the effect. At the peak of a move, time slows to a near freeze; the
camera **orbits the frozen moment**; bullets crawl through the air leaving rippling trails;
debris, casings and raindrops hang suspended; then everything snaps back to full speed. Between
those moments, the fighting is sleek, acrobatic and precise. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `low-key`, graded cold (green-cyan or steel blue) with hard highlights; sleek black
clothing, sunglasses, long coats. Lobbies with pillars, rooftops, subway platforms, rain.

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are medium to long** (3–5 s), because the frozen moment needs time to be circled.
- **Sizes**: medium and wide around the frozen moment (the whole body mid-air, the bullets in
  frame); extreme-close for a bullet or a drop of water.
- **Camera**: the **`Arc Shot`** is the style — at least one per fight, circling a subject frozen
  mid-move. `Push In` to a bullet; `Static Shot` for the snap back to speed.
- **Speed**: the freeze and the snap back are **two cuts** (one move per cut, gated): the frozen
  cut says `time slows to a near freeze as …, in slow motion`, the next cut says `time snaps back
  to full speed as …`. One freeze per sequence: too many and none of them lands.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the dodge** | wide: the gunman fires across the rooftop (shot) → medium, Arc Shot: time freezes as she bends backwards, bullets crawling past above her with rippling trails (dodge, slow motion) → wide: time snaps back and she springs upright (consequence) |
| **the frozen kick** | medium: he leaps into a spinning kick (leap) → medium, Arc Shot: time freezes mid-air, the camera circling him, rain suspended (kick, slow motion) → close: the kick lands at full speed (kick, impact) → wide: the opponent crashes through a pillar (consequence) |
| **the lobby** | wide: the pillared lobby, guards turning (establish) → medium: she fires, casings hanging in the air in slow motion (shot, impact) → wide: pillar fragments exploding and hanging, then falling (consequence) |
| **the bullet** | extreme-close: a bullet crawling through the air, the air rippling around it (shot, slow motion) → close: his raised hand, the bullet stopping in front of his palm (power) |

## Sound

The world slowing: sounds stretching and dropping in pitch, a deep boom, a single heartbeat, then
everything snapping back loud. Music: electronic, pulsing; `drop` into the freeze, `enter` hard on
the snap back.

## Example `motion.action`

- `she bends backwards as the bullets pass above her, in slow motion`
- `time freezes as he spins into the kick mid-air, in slow motion` (the next cut: `time snaps back to full speed as the kick lands`)
- `a bullet crawls toward his palm and stops in the air`

## Review questions

- Is there an Arc Shot around a frozen moment?
- Can you see the bullets, the casings, the rain hanging in the air?
- Does time snap back hard after the freeze?
