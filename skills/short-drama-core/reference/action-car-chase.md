# Action style `car-chase` — 飞车追逐

**The chase:** speed you can feel, cut between **inside** (the driver's hands, eyes, the mirror)
and **outside** (the cars racing low past the camera, drifting through turns, near misses with
traffic), building to a crash or an escape. A manoeuvre is a move: one swerve, drift, ram or
crash per cut. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `none` for daylight, `low-key` for night streets (tail lights and neon streaking in the
windows, wet asphalt).

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are short**: 2–3 s, getting shorter toward the crash.
- **Sizes**: wide and extreme wide outside (the cars in the street, the traffic, the turn);
  medium and close inside (the driver, the gear stick, the mirror with the pursuer in it).
- **Camera**: `Tracking Shot` low alongside the car (the style's shot), a static camera at road
  level as a car roars past, `Pan Left` / `Pan Right` following a drift, `Shake Slightly` inside
  the car.
- **Alternate inside and outside**: never three outside cuts in a row without the driver's face.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the start** | close: the driver's hand on the gear stick (establish) → wide, road level: the car tears away, tyres smoking (chase) → close: the mirror, headlights appearing behind (pressure) |
| **the drift** | wide, high: the car slides sideways through the intersection (swerve) → medium inside: the driver fighting the wheel (performance) → wide: the pursuer misses the turn (consequence) |
| **the near miss** | low tracking: the car weaves between two trucks (swerve) → close inside: her eyes (reaction) → wide: the truck's mirror clipped off (consequence) |
| **the ram** | medium: the pursuer rams the rear bumper (crash, impact) → medium inside: the driver thrown forward (reaction) |
| **the crash** | wide: the pursuer's car hits the barrier and rolls (crash, impact) → wide: it comes to rest upside down, wheels spinning (consequence) → close inside: the driver breathing, watching it in the mirror (reaction) |

## Staging

City streets at night, highways, tunnels, markets the car smashes through, mountain roads,
docks. Traffic is part of the choreography: trucks, buses, scooters, a crossing pedestrian.

## Sound

Engines rising and falling through the gears, tyres screeching, horns doppler-shifting past,
metal crunching, glass, then the silence after the crash. Music: driving; `drop` at the crash.

## Example `motion.action`

- `the car slides sideways through the intersection`
- `the black sedan rams the rear bumper`
- `she shifts down and floors the pedal`

## Review questions

- Is there a low tracking shot alongside the car?
- Do inside and outside alternate, with the driver's face every few cuts?
- Does the chase build to a crash, an escape or a stop?
