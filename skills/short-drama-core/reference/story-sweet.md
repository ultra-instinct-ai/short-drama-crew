# Story genre `sweet` — 甜宠

霸总甜宠 · 先婚后爱 · 契约婚姻 · 闪婚 · 豪门甜宠 · 古装甜宠 (追妻火葬场 is a neighbour with its own
ratios, §7). Female-audience content is over 70 % of 短剧 heat, 现言甜宠 is a top-3 genre on
Douyin, and about three quarters of ReelShort's audience is female. Shared machinery: `story.md`.

## 1. The promise — to be seen and chosen

The fantasy is being **seen and chosen** by someone who could have anyone, and mattering more to
them than their power: 被偏爱, 双标 (cold to the world, soft only to her). The genre signature is
**高糖低虐 + 强反差**. Three layers of sugar, and a good series gives all three and escalates them
from private to public:

| layer | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| 糖 | the single sweet moment (the protective arm, the jacket, the kiss) |
| 心动 | the tension before it (拉扯: 相爱不敢认，想近不敢亲，嘴硬心软，欲言又止) |
| 被偏爱 | the lasting reward: public, exclusive, unconditional favour |

The taste is moving from the domineering 霸总 to the **外冷内热 / devoted "puppy" male lead** — the
superior party bowing down for love — and from the 小白花 to a heroine who thinks for herself.
The power balance shifts over the series: he starts in control, they end as equals.

## 2. The series — milestones on time

The romance beat sheets agree on the shape (Gwen Hayes' four phases — set-up, falling in love,
retreating, fighting for love; Billy Mernit's Cute Meet → Hook → Swivel → Dark Moment → Joyful
Defeat; the K-drama confession at the midpoint). Compressed to 短剧 paywall realities (80 episodes):

| phase | episodes | milestones (`outline.romance[]`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| golden | 1–10 | **meet** in E1 and the **bind** by E3 (闪婚 / contract / 替嫁 / cohabitation / 婚约 — the device that keeps them together); the first **spark** and **touch** by E5; the first **protect** (护妻); the paywall (卡一 ~E8–12) on an emotional or identity hook |
| escalation A | 11–40 | forced proximity; **jealousy**; the first **kiss** around 25–35 %; he defends her in public; the rival escalates |
| midpoint | ~40–60 % | the **confession** — the "midpoint of love", a relationship acknowledged or a 隐婚 half-revealed |
| escalation B | to ~70 % | the family obstacle, 掉马 (an identity reveal), doubt; short **misunderstandings** that clear within 2–3 episodes |
| climax | last 25 % | **one** short **black moment** at 70–90 %, then **reconcile** within 5 episodes, the grand gesture, **public** (公开 / 官宣 / wedding), the obstacles 打脸-ed, an epilogue of sugar — **commit** |

Milestone vocabulary: `meet` `bind` `spark` `touch` `jealousy` `protect` `kiss` `confession`
`misunderstanding` (with `until`, the episode it clears) `reconcile` `public` `black-moment`
`commit`.

**The rules** (gate `story-sweet`; researched defaults, waivable on the user's request):

| rule | default | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sweet-share` | ≥ 40 % of episodes pay in sugar (强宠 / 情感释放); ≥ 25 % when mixed | 高糖低虐; producers literally count kisses |
| `sweet-gap` | ≤ 3 episodes in a row without sugar (4 in the climax; +2 when mixed) | 误会 cycles close in 2–3 episodes; "虐后快速给甜" |
| `meet-bind` | meet in E1, bind by E3 | episode 1 ties the leads together; without the bind they drift apart |
| `first-touch` | touch (or kiss) by E5 (runs ≥ 10) | show the promise before the viewer pays |
| `first-kiss` | by half the run (runs ≥ 10) | Hayes' deepening-desire phase; the K-drama midpoint |
| `midpoint-confession` | a confession at 40–60 % (runs ≥ 20) | the midpoint of love |
| `misunderstanding-run` | each clears within 3 episodes (5 in the climax); only one may run past 3 | "a misunderstanding one sentence could clear" loses the audience |
| `black-moment` | at most one, at 70–90 %, mended within 5 episodes | Break Up → Grand Gesture; Dark Moment → Joyful Defeat |
| `he-ending` | sugar in one of the last three episodes | the genre promises a happy ending |

`story-advice` also notes a climax without a **public** milestone and 强宠 payoffs that are mostly
money (黑卡 / 买下 / 豪车 — regulators target 拜金 / 炫富 / 炫权 爽点).

## 3. The episode

- **Hook** in the first seconds, readable with the sound off: tension or friction between the
  leads, or a rival's pressure.
- **The sugar lands in the back half, just before the cliff** (the 7-3-1 model: setup, high sugar,
  a sting of suspense). Sweet shots are held longer (3–6 s, close) than 打脸 shots.
- **A skeleton**: 0–10 % hook → 10–60 % pressure (rival, family, situation) → 60–90 % 撒糖 or
  护妻 → 90–100 % a new question.
- **Cliffs**: 情感 (the almost-kiss interrupted; "你吃醋了？" unanswered; he overhears her plan to
  leave), 身份 (his identity or the 隐婚 about to be exposed), 危机 (the rival's or the family's
  ambush), 反转 (the contract was his long-planned choice — 蓄谋已久). Romance cliffs work best when
  desire and status collide in public.
- **Plan one or two shareable 名场面** per series (the 抵墙吻) — they drive 二创.

## 4. Characters

- **The male lead** is a *position*, not a job title — anyone 上位者 (CEO, general, prince,
  lawyer, 大佬). His harshness is aimed at others, never humiliating her; his possessive acts
  protect, never control. Overseas audiences reject possessiveness framed as love, non-consent and
  humiliation.
- **The female lead** is independent and has wins of her own (she lands some 打脸 / 智商碾压 — not
  only rescued). Avoid the greasy heroine (exaggerated coquetry, over-explicit flirting).
- **The map**: the rival 女二 (schemer or 白月光, paid off by 打脸); the gentle 男二 who must not
  out-care the lead (second-lead syndrome — give him a noble exit); the family obstacle (豪门
  mother, stepmother, elders); an ally or comic relief (assistant, best friend, 萌宝). Keep the
  core to 4–6.
- **Avoid**: a 一夜情 opening; money as the sweet payoff; the male lead as the main antagonist
  (only in 追妻火葬场).

## 5. The tropes, kept fresh

Staples: 壁咚 / 抵墙吻, 公主抱, the piggyback, 包扎伤口, 披外套, 吃醋, 护妻 (撑腰 in public), 双标,
forced proximity (同住 / 一张床), 隐婚 → 公开, 掉马, the contract deadline, the family-dinner defence.

- **缺啥给啥** — small, specific, personal details beat grand gestures.
- **Borrowed intimacy** — the fake couple's staged touches become dangerous because they are real;
  a contract is a slow burn with a deadline: use the clock.
- **Stack a primary and a secondary trope** (contract marriage + hidden identity); mix genres
  (萌宝, 年代, 古装, 奇幻).
- **Run each trope once privately and later publicly, and flip who does it** (she protects him,
  she makes him jealous). Never the same trope in consecutive sweet episodes.

## 6. Dialogue

- **拉扯**: cold words, soft details (口是心非); the truth in micro-expressions, eye contact and small
  actions. Care through action and one-line understatement ("手给我"), not speeches.
- **句句不骚，句句都撩.** "关系没到位，再高级的情话也会变成油腻" — match the line to the relationship
  stage: no "你是我的" before the midpoint. Keep 土味情话 for comic self-awareness.
- **Banter carries status play**: she wins verbal rounds.

## 7. What gets rejected — and 追妻火葬场

Rejections: old patterns (90 % of scripts cut at first review for 模式老套); everyone stupid
(全员降智); no 拉扯, flat chemistry ("chemistry matters more than plot"); misunderstandings a line
would clear; too long 虐; a toxic, controlling or violent male lead; 炫富 / 拜金 payoffs; greasy
flirting; 工业糖水 sameness.

**追妻火葬场** (the chase after he hurt her) runs about 30 % 虐, 40 % his regret, 30 % the chase —
his regret must outweigh her suffering; she is soft early and strong later. Waive
`misunderstanding-run` / `sweet-gap` for it explicitly (`storyExceptions`), on the user's word.
