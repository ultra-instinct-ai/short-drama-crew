# Action style `zombie-horde` — 丧尸围城 / 末日求生

**The fight:** survivors against a horde. Not a duel and not a monster held back — the infected are
many, seen, and keep coming. Readability comes from **geography**: where the horde is, the choke
point, the barricade, the way out. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`; the scare rules it combines
with: `grammar-horror.md` (`threat.kind: horde`).

**Look:** `horror` (a quiet outbreak at night) or `apocalypse` (the world after). `init` picks the
style from 丧尸 / 感染者 / zombie + 动作 / 求生 / 围城, or a zombie apocalypse (末日丧尸).

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are short to medium**: 2.5–4 s. Cut the segment `["horror", "action"]` when it is also a
  scare (the horde bursting through), or `action` alone for the open fight.
- **Open wide and high** on the geography: the stairwell, the corridor, the bus, the barricade, and
  the horde as one mass (a signature shot, advisory). Reset to it after each push.
- **Inside the fight**: handheld among the survivors (`Shake Slightly`, `Shake Strongly` at the
  breach); one move per cut — a swing, a shove, a door slammed, a barricade braced.
- **The horde moves as one**: a surge, a wave over a car, bodies piling against glass. Write the
  mass, not individual choreography: `the infected surge down the stairwell as one mass`.

## Violence — the bite by its consequence

Providers refuse gore, and on the mainland it is a red line (`init` sets `violence: implied` for a
zombie genre there). Never write bites into flesh, wounds, entrails or dismemberment. Show the
**consequence**: a sleeve torn, the bitten one hiding the arm, a friend's face as they understand,
the infected one's eyes clouding (`story-horror.md` §7; on the mainland the infection is 科学 — a
virus, an experiment — and the wording is 感染者 / 变异者).

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the barricade** | wide, high: the horde fills the street below the window (establish) → medium, handheld: two survivors drag a cabinet against the door (grapple) → close: the door shudders, a gap of grey fingers (pressure) → medium: the cabinet holds (consequence) |
| **the choke point** | wide down the corridor: he stands in the doorway, the horde coming (establish) → medium: he swings the fire axe handle into the first (strike, impact) → medium: it falls back into the others (reaction) → wide: the mass keeps coming (consequence) |
| **the bite** | close: her hand pulling her sleeve down over her forearm (performance) → close: his face, seeing it (reaction) |

## Sound

The horde's mass sound (a low roar of groans, shuffling, bodies on glass), the barricade's creak,
breath, a radio, the sudden silence when the door holds. Music: a driving low pulse, out before the
breach.

## Review questions

- Can the audience point at the horde, the choke point and the way out?
- Does the horde read as one mass?
- Is there any gore on screen? Show the consequence instead.
