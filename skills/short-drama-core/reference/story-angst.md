# Story genre `angst` — 虐恋 / 追妻火葬场

虐恋情深 · 追妻火葬场 · 替身文学 · 白月光 / 朱砂痣 · 带球跑 · 先虐后甜. The opposite pole of `sweet`
(`story-sweet.md`); in a mixed drama the two share the romance, and `angst` decides the shape.
Shared machinery: `story.md`.

## 1. The promise — delayed justice in love

The audience does not pay to suffer. **The pain is a deposit; his regret and pursuit are the
withdrawal, with interest.** 追妻火葬场 is a power reversal: early on he controls the relationship,
later she sets the price of forgiveness — "emotional fairness", his arrogance collapsing into
regret. A 虐点 that is never repaid is a broken promise, not a stronger drama.

- **Make them love her before anything breaks.** Tragedy is destroying something beautiful in
  front of people who care. Her devotion is concrete (the hangover soup at 3 a.m., the three-year
  diary, the career she gave up).
- **Catharsis** swings between "too late" (tears) and "in the nick of time" (action). 火葬场 is
  built on "almost too late": a BE lands on too late, a HE on the nick of time.
- **The market is moving**: 女频 dominates the billion-play titles and 虐恋 with 追妻 reversals was a
  2025 mainstay, but toward the **清醒女主** and the **克制霸总**; the 壁咚 / 强吻 era is over. 虐 survives
  as 甜虐 with a dignified heroine, not as prolonged victimhood.

## 2. The series — 虐 ≤ 30 %, 悔 ≈ 40 %, 追 ≈ 30 %

| phase | where (80 episodes) | what |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 虐 | to ~30 % | cause and effect, not wallowing: her devotion, his false belief, the wound; **the heroine's dark night** (she loses the child, the marriage, her illusions) and **she leaves by her own choice** — the 卡一 cliff (E8–12) ends on that decision |
| 悔 | ~25–45 % → | **the turn**: he learns or suspects the truth, or she "dies" / disappears — the 卡二 cliff (E26–30); **the hero's dark night** (too late, she is gone) |
| 追 | → ~85 % | the 火葬场: she holds power; every pursuit beat costs him something he values (status, pride in public, money, his family, his body in the rain); a public apology before witnesses; the antagonist who engineered it all is paid off |
| ending | last ~15 % | HE (先虐后甜 — sugar after), HE with the second lead (男二上位, when he crossed a line), BE (declared, prestige), OE (rare). Whatever the ending, she ends with more agency than she began |

**Tracked in the outline** (gate `story-angst`): `outline.angst: { ending, turnEp, exitEp,
reconcileEp, misbelief: { belief, engineeredBy, evidence, audienceRevealEp, heroRevealEp },
beats: [{ ep, kind: 虐 / 悔 / 追 / 糖, target?, inflictor?, what?, cost?, severity?, releaseEp?,
public? }] }` and `outline.brief.redLines` (what the male lead never does).

| rule | default | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `abuse-cap` | the last 虐 beat before the turn at ≤ 35 % | 虐妻 ≤ 30 %, with room for the 卡二 gate |
| `turn-timing` | the turn at 25–45 % | regret begins at 卡二 |
| `regret-outweighs` | 悔 + 追 beats ≥ 1.5 × 虐 beats | "his regret must outweigh her suffering" (30/40/30 is 2.3×) |
| `pursuit-length` | ≥ 3 追 beats, lasting ≥ 15 % before she forgives (HE) | 火葬场不够烧 is the standard complaint |
| `release-cadence` | every 虐 beat released within 2 episodes | swipe behaviour; the 虐/甜/爽 rotation |
| `engineered-misbelief` | the misunderstanding is engineered by a non-lead, rests on evidence, the audience knows by E3, he learns by 55 % | a misunderstanding one sentence would clear reads as 降智; the tears come from irony |
| `exit-by-choice` | she leaves at 10–40 %, no later than the turn | her leaving is her choice, not an expulsion |
| `costly-regret` | every 悔 / 追 beat has a cost; one public 追 beat | the grovel is before witnesses |
| `ending-contract` | HE reconciles at ≥ 85 % with sugar after; a BE brief does not promise sweetness | 先虐后甜 is the commercial default |
| `male-lead-red-lines` | `brief.redLines` declared; no beat where the male lead strikes, drugs, confines, coerces or forces a kneel | 虐 is emotional and situational; regulators and audiences refuse the rest |

## 3. The episode

- A minor climax every 2–3 minutes, a cliff at every end, the core conflict inside 30 s.
- **Every 虐 episode still has a 爽 beat — her dignity**: a cold line, a refusal, the divorce papers
  signed, a walk-out, a competence the audience sees. Not him being kind.
- **Dramatic irony is the engine**: the audience sees her hidden sacrifice, he does not. The
  flashback that re-reads his cruelty is the tearjerker loop.
- **Hooks by phase**: 虐 — 情感 (her silent breakdown), 结果 (the test, the diagnosis), 道具 (the diary,
  the ultrasound); 悔 — 反转 (he finds the evidence), 身份 (she was the real 白月光 / the benefactor);
  追 — 危机 (a rival suitor, the villainess strikes again), 情感 (will she take his hand).

## 4. Characters

- **The male lead** wounds her severely but for a reason: 爱而不自知, a misbelief planted by a third
  party, or treating her as a 替身. His cruelty is consistent with a false belief. Never
  irredeemable; never physical abuse (`redLines`).
- **The heroine** may suffer but is never 圣母 or 降智; her silence has a reason (a promise, pride,
  shielding someone, no proof); "redeemed only after her death" is criticised — she lives to set
  the price.
- **The 白月光 / 替身 structure**: A loves C (the unreachable first love); B loves A and resembles C.
  Modern variants: the 替身 was the real childhood saviour; the 白月光 manufactured the claim; the 替身
  walks away and C's return disappoints him. The 朱砂痣 — the one who stayed — is her position.
- **The engine** needs a named inflictor (the 白月光, a mother-in-law, a rival), an artifact of
  evidence, and a reveal the audience sees early; the inflictor is paid off (打脸 / 复仇) before the
  reconciliation.

## 5. Beats and freshness

| beat | status |
| --- | --- |
| 带球跑 (she leaves pregnant, returns years later with a 萌宝) | alive; needs a time skip and a transformed heroine; child rules apply (`story-family.md`) |
| 死遁 / 假死 (he goes mad) | alive; give her a point of view in the gap years |
| 挖肾 / 献血 / 换心 | dated and restricted — blood and surgery close-ups are banned on platforms; imply |
| 失忆 | usable as a clock or reversal; mocked as a deus ex machina |
| terminal illness / a clock | strong, if planted early |
| 离婚协议 / 流产 | a powerful 卡点 — the aftermath, never gore |
| 雨中下跪, the public grovel | popular when *he* chooses it; forcing anyone to kneel, slaps, thrown money are banned |
| 强吻 / 壁咚 / 强制爱 | dated and targeted — avoid |

Give each lead a signature line early and reprise it with the roles swapped: the echo is the
payoff.

## 6. Dialogue

Lines under 10 characters. The 虐 phase runs on understatement — her pain in subtext ("好。" "我签。"
"你开心就好。"), his cruelty in cold certainty ("别让我再说第二遍。"). The 悔 phase reverses the register:
he halts and repeats ("我错了"; "你回来好不好"), she is crisp and final. No monologues — tears and a
prop close-up do the work.

## 7. What fails, and the red lines

A 虐 phase too long; a purely evil male lead; cheap forgiveness; pain for its own sake; a 圣母
heroine; misunderstandings that need stupidity; a new misunderstanding after the truth is out; the
白月光 never punished; a reconciliation nobody witnesses. **Regulation**: NRTA "爽而有度" (2025), the
correct 婚恋观 in the 2024–25 notices, the 霸总 correction (2024, 2025), the June 2026 campaign on
畸形婚恋观 and 暴力复仇; platform rules (红果 2026) ban forced kneeling, slaps, thrown money, blood
close-ups, knives to the throat and drugging. `story-advice` flags organ harvesting, blood and
forced kisses in the outline.
