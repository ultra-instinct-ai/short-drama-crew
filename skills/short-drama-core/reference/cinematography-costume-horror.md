# Cinematography preset `costume-horror` — 古代恐怖 / 古装灵异 / 聊斋志怪 / 民俗古装 / 僵尸

The look: **the `horror` look in a world lit only by fire and the sky.** Most of the frame is black;
one flame — a candle carried down a corridor, an oil lamp, a paper lantern, a dying brazier — or
moonlight through a lattice or a torn paper window carves the space out of it. Everything in
`cinematography-horror.md` applies (the dark frame, faces partly lit, the threat in shadow, the
beautifiers refused, supernatural light kept small); this guide adds what changes before electric
light. How a scare is cut: `grammar-horror.md`.

`init` picks it when a horror genre is also a period one: 古代 / 古装 / 古风 / 聊斋 / 志怪 / 僵尸 /
宫廷 / 江湖 / 武侠 / 仙侠. A 民国 ghost story stays `horror` (the era had bulbs and torches). It is
**not** `costume-dark` with a ghost: `costume-dark` is history lit by fire, composed for the
court's scale; `costume-horror` is the dark the fire cannot reach.

## 1. Light — the one flame

| source | 中文 | what it does | how to write it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a candle carried | 手持蜡烛 | the moving source: the light walks with the person, the corridor behind them closes up | `lit only by the candle in her hand, the corridor behind her falling to black within a few steps` |
| a paper lantern | 纸灯笼 | a soft orb with a hard edge; swinging, it makes shadows lurch | `the paper lantern swings, its pool of light sliding across the wet flagstones` |
| a guttering flame | 将熄的火 | the failing source — the period flicker: see / not see | `the oil lamp gutters; in each dark beat the far end of the hall disappears` |
| moonlight through lattice | 窗棂月光 | bars of cold light across a face or a floor, black between them | `cold moonlight through the lattice lays bars across the floor, the rest of the room black` |
| a torn paper window | 破窗纸 | a slit of light; the silhouette behind paper — the period's best way to show a shape | `a silhouette moves behind the paper screen, lit from the other side` |
| the flame from below | 底光 | the low candle on the floor or a table: eye sockets in shadow | `lit from below by the candle on the floor, eye sockets in shadow` |

- **Nothing electric** (gated: `light.source` refuses bulbs, tubes, neon, screens, phones and
  street lights). A flashlight is the modern horror's searching beam; here the searching light is the
  carried candle or the lantern.
- **The flame is the only warmth.** Cold blue-grey night, the flame a small amber point; never a
  warm golden room. Keep one accent: talisman yellow, a red wedding lantern, blood red lacquer.
- **Ritual light stays small**: a talisman flame, a seal's glow, a bronze mirror's glint lights
  only what it touches (gated, as in `horror`).

## 2. Composition — paper, wood and doorways

The period house is built of layers that hide things: paper screens (纸屏风), bead and gauze
curtains, lattice doors, a well in the courtyard, the coffin in the hall, the altar's ancestor
tablets, a long covered corridor (回廊). Use them to block the view, to put a silhouette behind
paper, to leave a dark doorway at the end of a hall.

- **Back to the danger, dead space behind** — the same devices as `horror`, with period doors.
- **Symmetry** only when it is wrong: the ancestor hall's rows of tablets, a bridal chamber set out
  too perfectly, a corridor of identical lanterns. A balanced court frame reads as `costume-dark`.
- **No petals, gauze glow or mist-in-sunlight** — those are `costume-idol`.

## 3. Threat and motion

The period monster is usually still, slow and *wrong*: the bride who sits too straight, the
hopping 僵尸 (shown in cuts, never as a dance), the figure behind the paper screen, the hair in the
well. Appear and disappear between gusts that blow the candle out. On the mainland, `story-horror.md`
§7: the threat is explained by a human, a hallucination or a fraud — period settings get more
leeway, but never a ghost confirmed real.

## 4. Review questions

- Is every light a flame or the sky? Remove anything that would need electricity.
- Is the flame the only warmth in the frame?
- What does the paper, the lattice or the curtain hide in this shot?
- Is this a court drama with a ghost (too composed, too lit)? Darken it.
