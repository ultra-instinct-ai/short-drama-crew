# Action style `horror-struggle` — 恐怖搏斗

**The fight:** a struggle against something stronger, not a duel. The exorcist, the survivor, the
apprentice is outmatched and survives by a rule, a ritual or luck — never by technique. Short,
ugly, confused; the threat is partly unseen; the light swings. Shared rules and fields:
`action.md`; the horror grammar it combines with: `grammar-horror.md`.

**Look:** `horror` (`cinematography-horror.md`). Never with `wuxia-fantasy` or `superhero`: wire
flight, sword-light trails, swirling qi and glowing beams turn the scare into a fantasy fight (the
`params` gate refuses the combination).

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are short to medium**: 2.5–4 s. Cut the segment `["horror", "action"]`: the fight
  rules (open wide, one move per cut, a reaction after every impact) and the horror rules (the
  hold before the scare, the still reveal) both apply.
- **Sizes**: medium and close, cramped; one wide to open, mostly black.
- **Camera**: handheld (`Shake Slightly`, `Shake Strongly` at the worst), in the middle of it.
- **Readability comes from one clear image per beat** — the talisman hitting the lid, the hand on
  the throat — not from wide choreography. Signature shots (advisory): a handheld cut, a close
  insert on that one image.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the grab** | wide, mostly black: the flashlight swings across the room (establish) → close, handheld: a pale hand closes on his ankle (grapple, impact) → medium: he falls hard onto the floorboards (fall, reaction) |
| **the charm** | close: the paper charm in his shaking fingers (pressure) → extreme-close: the charm slaps onto the wet lid and sticks (strike, impact) → medium, static: the lid stops moving; his breath (consequence) |
| **the loss** | medium: the candle goes out in the struggle (consequence) → close: only his eyes, lit by the phone on the floor (reaction) |

## Staging and props

Narrow rooms, stairwells, a bathroom, the space under a house. Ritual tools stay physical: fire,
sparks, smoke, a mirror's glint, a paper charm sticking to a wet surface, salt, a bell. Their light
lights only what it touches.

## Sound

Breath, scraping, a body on wood, the charm's paper, the bell, the flashlight clattering. Music:
none, or a low drone that drops out before the hit.

## Example `motion.action`

- `a pale hand closes around his ankle and pulls`
- `he slaps the paper charm onto the wet lid`
- `the candle goes out as he falls`

## Review questions

- Is the hero outmatched? Does each exchange end worse for them, even when they "win" it?
- Is the threat only half-seen, and lit only by the swinging light?
- Does any frame look like wuxia?
