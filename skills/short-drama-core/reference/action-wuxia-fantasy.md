# Action style `wuxia-fantasy` — 仙侠武侠

**The fight:** poetry, not brawling. Fighters glide and float on wires, run up walls and across
water, trade blows on bamboo tips and rooftops; sleeves, hair and ribbons flow; swords leave
trails of light; qi and spells swirl as visible energy. Slow motion and freeze poses turn a clash
into a painting. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `costume-idol` (luminous, gauze, mist, petals). With `costume-dark`, the same style
becomes a darker 武侠: smoke instead of petals, moonlight instead of sun.

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are medium to long**: 3–5 s, room for a leap to rise and land.
- **Sizes**: wides and extreme wides for flight (the figure small against the sky, the lake, the
  bamboo sea) — they also serve the `costume-idol` 大全景 rule; mediums for the exchange; close-ups
  for the eyes before the strike and for the sword.
- **Camera**: `Pedestal Up` / `Tilt Up` rising with a leap, a slow `Arc Shot` around two fighters
  locked blade to blade, `Tracking Shot` running alongside a flight across rooftops.
- **Speed**: slow motion is the style. The leap, the sword stroke, the moment the spell releases:
  write `in slow motion`. A freeze pose (定格亮相) ends the big move.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **bamboo tips** | extreme wide: two figures on the tips of a swaying bamboo sea (establish) → medium: she leaps across, in slow motion (leap) → medium: their blades meet, sparks and a ring of light (strike, impact) → wide: the bamboo whips back as they separate (consequence) |
| **walking on water** | wide: he runs across the lake, each step a ripple (chase) → medium: he spins and draws, sword light trailing (draw) → close: the pursuer's face as the light reaches him (reaction) |
| **the sword light** | medium: she raises the sword, qi gathering along the blade (power) → wide: one stroke, a crescent of light crossing the courtyard (slash, impact) → wide: the pillars split and fall (consequence) |
| **the freeze** | after the decisive stroke, a medium: both fighters hold their final poses, back to back, the petals still falling around them (stance) |

## Staging

Bamboo forests, lakes, cliffs, rooftops of a water town, snowy peaks, temple courtyards, a
lantern-lit bridge. Air is never empty: petals, leaves, snow, mist moved by the fight.

## Sound

Wind, the ring of blades, sword chimes, the whoosh of flight, a low hum as qi builds. Music:
guqin, erhu, drums; `swell` on the slow-motion leap, `silence` before the final stroke.

## Example `motion.action`

- `she leaps from the bamboo tip in slow motion, sleeves spread`
- `he draws the sword and a trail of blue light follows the blade`
- `their blades meet and a ring of light bursts outward`

## Review questions

- Does anyone fly? Is there a wide that shows the height and the space?
- Is the decisive move in slow motion, and does it end in a pose?
- Is the air moving — petals, leaves, mist?
