# Action style `gun-fu` — 近身枪斗

**The fight:** a professional working. Guns at arm's length, fused with throws, joint locks and
takedowns; every move efficient, nothing wasted. The camera stays back and **lets the whole body
be seen** in long takes, so the audience believes the skill. Violence is fast, clean and
procedural: reload, clear the room, move on. Shared rules and fields: `action.md`.

**Look:** `low-key` (neon, wet streets, a club, a hotel corridor, a concrete stairwell). Colour
from practicals: magenta and cyan neon, sodium orange, muzzle flash.

## Rhythm and coverage

- **Cuts are long**: 4–5 s is normal (average 3.5–5 s). Fewer, longer cuts than any other style.
- **Sizes are wide and medium**: the body in the frame, head to shoes. Close-ups are for the
  gun (a reload insert) and the eyes (the decision), not for the blows.
- **Camera**: `Tracking Shot` following the fighter down a corridor, `Truck Left` / `Truck Right`
  alongside a room clear, `Static Shot` wides for an exchange. Almost no shake: the camera is as
  controlled as the fighter.
- **Speed**: real time. No slow motion except, rarely, the last shot of a sequence.

## Signature sequences

| sequence | cuts |
| --- | --- |
| **the room clear** | wide: he enters, three men turn (establish) → medium tracking: he fires at the first (shot, impact) → wide: the man drops (consequence) → medium: he throws the second over his hip (throw, impact) → close: the second hits the floor (reaction) → medium: he fires down at him (shot) |
| **the reload** | medium: slide locks back, empty → extreme-close insert: the magazine drops and the new one slams in (reload) → medium: he raises the gun as the next man rounds the corner |
| **gun and grapple** | medium: the enemy grabs his gun arm (grapple) → medium: he twists the arm into a lock (grapple) → close: the enemy's face hits the wall (reaction) |
| **the stairwell** | high wide down the stairwell: the fighters on two landings (establish) → medium: he fires up through the gap (shot) → wide: a body falls past the rail (consequence) |

## Staging and props

Corridors, stairwells, clubs, glass rooms, car parks: spaces with lines and cover. Tactical
suits, holsters, suppressors. Spent casings on the floor are the style's detail, and each fight
ends in a room of stillness and smoke.

## Sound

Suppressed and unsuppressed shots with real weight, casings tinkling, the slide racking, bodies
hitting floors, heavy breath. Music: a driving low synth or bass under the whole fight
(`enter` on the first shot, `drop` for the final one).

## Example `motion.action` (one move each)

- `he fires twice into the chest of the man at the bar` (one move: the shot)
- `he hooks the man's arm and throws him over his hip onto the tiles`
- `the empty magazine drops and he slams in a new one`

## Review questions

- Can you see the whole body in the blows? Is the cut long enough to believe it?
- Does each cut hold one move, and does the next cut show what it did?
- Is there a reload, a grapple and a moment of stillness after?
