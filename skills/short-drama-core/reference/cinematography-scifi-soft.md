# Cinematography preset `scifi-soft` — 软科幻 · 赛博朋克 · 未来都市

The look: **the future as feeling.** Holograms float over streets, neon and glowing interfaces
colour every face, the air is thick with haze that catches beams of light, and rain turns the
ground into a mirror. Technology is imaginative, sleek and unexplained; the story is about the
people inside it. Two poles: **neon dystopia** (cyan and magenta, rain, crowds, 赛博朋克) and
**luminous utopia** (white, glass, soft gradients, gardens in towers). The mechanics shared by
every preset are in `cinematography.md`.

## 1. Light — colour you can see — `light.source`, `light.direction`

Every cut names its glowing source, carried verbatim in the frame with `luminous soft science
fiction lighting`.

| place | the source | what it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| night street | neon signs, holographic billboards, shop screens | dual-tone colour on the face (cyan one side, magenta the other), reflections in the wet ground |
| apartment | a window of city light, a hologram companion, a glowing interface | colour through haze, the face lit by the interface from below and the side |
| club, market | strobes, colour washes, light strips | saturated pools and beams through smoke |
| utopia | glass ceilings, light panels, bioluminescent plants | soft gradients, white and pastel, glowing edges |
| energy tech | an energy core, a weapon charging, a portal | a moving coloured key, bloom and flare |

- **Dual-tone light is the signature**: two coloured sources, one on each side of the face. Key
  from the side or behind (`side-*`, `back-*`, `backlight`: at least half the cuts with people,
  gated); **never** `frontal` or `ambient` (the colour flattens).
- **Haze in every frame** so the beams show; rain or wet ground for reflections.
- **Scene lighting states** contain `luminous soft science fiction lighting` and the sources:
  > `luminous soft science fiction lighting: magenta and cyan neon signs stacked up the walls of the narrow street, a holographic koi drifting above the crossing, haze catching the beams, the wet ground reflecting every colour. Empty street, no people.`

## 2. Colour and design

- **Neon dystopia**: cyan, magenta, violet, acid green against black; grime, crowds, cables,
  vending machines, noodle stalls under holograms.
- **Luminous utopia**: white, glass, pale blue and gold, soft gradients, plants and water in
  architecture.
- **Design is imaginative**: floating screens, transparent phones, drones, robots with faces,
  light-lined clothing. Pick 3–5 signature devices per world and make them anchors so they recur.
- **Wardrobe**: layered street wear with light strips, clear raincoats, sleek utopian uniforms.

## 3. Composition and camera

- **The city is a character**: extreme wides of the skyline and the street canyon; the person
  small under the billboards. Vertical 9:16 suits towers and stacked signs.
- **Reflections and layers**: shoot through wet glass, holograms and screens in the foreground
  (soft, glowing).
- **Camera**: gliding: slow `Tracking Shot` through the crowd, `Pedestal Up` up a tower, `Arc Shot`
  around a hologram; drones as camera positions.
- **Lens**: 35–85mm, T1.5–T2, so the neon turns into large coloured bokeh.

## 4. With other axes

- `action: bullet-time` or `superhero` for 未来动作; `grammar: faceslap` for a 逆袭 in a
  corporate tower; `comedy: romcom` for a robot romance; `composition: art-first` for a
  contemplative future.

## 5. Sound

Rain, crowds in many languages, synth hums, holographic chimes, drones passing. Music: synthwave,
ambient pads.

## 6. Review questions

- Are there two colours on the face?
- Is there haze for the beams and a wet surface for the reflections?
- Are the world's signature devices in the frame?
- Is the city felt — a wide that shows how big it is?
