# Content craft — hooks, loops, overlays, captions, covers, carousels

What makes a post perform once the size is right: the first three seconds, the ending that
re-plays, text that stays inside the safe zone, a caption shaped for each platform's reading
pattern, a posting slot to start from, a cover that reads at thumbnail size, and a carousel that
tells one story. Sizes and limits are quoted from platforms-global.md and platforms-china.md,
not restated here; teaser and poster recipes built on these rules are in repurpose.md.

## The first 3 seconds

Feeds decide in the first swipe-length. The product, the face or the question is on screen by
second 3, in motion, with no logo card, no fade-in and no "hi guys". Hook types, with a line
each (drama and product examples side by side):

| Hook type | Drama teaser | Product / brand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shocking line, mid-scene | 「你以为我不知道孩子是谁的？」 cut in on the reaction | "This is the last one we could get made." |
| Result first, cause later | the slap, the arrest, the reveal — then 「三天前…」 | the after-state (clean pan, finished room), then the before |
| Direct question | 「如果你婆婆当众撕了你的结婚证，你会怎么做？」 | "Why does every white sneaker turn yellow in a month?" |
| Contradiction / mismatch | the bride in a wedding dress signing divorce papers | a luxury unboxing on a bus |
| Countdown / stakes | 「还有十分钟，她就要嫁给别人了」 | "Three tests. One survives." |
| Visual anomaly | one impossible detail in an ordinary frame (a wet footprint indoors) | the product doing the one thing rivals cannot |
| Named audience | 「二婚的姐妹看过来」 | "If you edit on a phone, watch this." |

Rules: one hook per cut, the first frame is already the strongest frame (it is also the cover),
sound-off comprehension — the hook line is burned in as text. Write 2–3 hooks for the same body
and let the platform pick (repurpose.md, variants).

## Loop-friendly endings

Reels, TikTok and 抖音 count re-watches. End on a frame that matches the first frame (same
composition, same subject position) so the loop hides the seam; or end one beat *before* the
answer the hook promised, so the viewer re-watches to catch it; or end on the CTA card that is
also the cover. Never end on a logo fade or a black frame. For a drama teaser the ending is the
cut before the payoff plus the line 「主页看全集 / full episode in bio」 (repurpose.md).

## On-screen text and burned subtitles

- Keep every word inside the safe zone of the 9:16 diagram (platforms-global.md): headline in the
  upper safe band, subtitles at the lower edge of the safe band (≈y 1100–1240 on 1080×1920), never
  behind the caption or the right rail.
- Subtitles are burned in (most feeds autoplay muted): 2 lines maximum, ≤ 14 CJK chars or ≤ 32
  Latin chars per line, high-contrast box or stroke, one style for the whole account.
- Headline type ≥ 60 px on 1080×1920, ≤ 6 words / 8 chars; a second overlay only when the first
  has left. Do not repeat the caption on screen.
- Text is added after the crop and at the final ratio (ratio-map.md, crop anchoring); text
  generated inside the picture is only for covers that are never cropped again.
- 「AI生成」 label where required (platforms-china.md) is the platform's toggle, not an overlay
  you design — but do not place other text where the platform paints its label (top-left on most
  Chinese apps).

## Caption structure per platform

| Platform | Structure | Hashtags / tags | Length notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | hook line (shown before "more") → 2–4 short lines → CTA (save / share / comment) | **≤5** (Dec 2025 cap) — 3 niche + 2 broad | 2,200 max; the first 125 chars are the post |
| TikTok / 抖音 | one-line hook + a question that invites comments | 3–5 hashtags / 话题, one is the series tag | 4,000 max on TikTok; 抖音 shows ≈20 chars of title |
| YouTube | title ≤ 60–70 chars shown; description: 2-line summary, links, chapters, disclosure | 3–5 hashtags at the end of the description | title 100 hard cap |
| Facebook | short hook, link in the first comment or the post, CTA | 1–3 | shares outweigh hashtags |
| X | the hook is the whole post; thread for detail | 1–2 or none | 280 |
| LinkedIn | hook in the first two lines → line-broken story → question → 3 hashtags | 3 | 3,000; "see more" after ≈3 lines |
| Pinterest | keyword title ≤ 100, description with 2–3 search phrases | none | description 500–800 *(verify)* |
| Threads | one sentence; the picture carries the rest | none | 500 |
| 小红书 | 标题 ≤ 20 with the keyword first → 正文 in short lines with emoji markers → 话题 tags | keyword + 3–6 话题 (#…#) | 正文 1,000 (长文 6,000) |
| 视频号 / 公众号 | one-line 描述 with 话题; the article's first line is the hook | 1–3 话题 | limits not confirmed |
| B站 | 标题 with the series and episode number; 简介 with timestamps | tags on upload (platform field) | limits not confirmed |

A CTA is one verb: 关注 / follow, 主页看全集 / full episode in bio, save, comment the answer.
Platform buttons (shop, link, subscribe) are not drawn into the picture.

## Posting-time starting points

Sprout Social 2026 aggregates, to be treated as **starting points** and replaced by the
account's own insights after two weeks: Instagram Mon–Thu 10:00–15:00; TikTok Wed 14:00–17:00;
LinkedIn Tue 11:00–17:00. Local time of the audience, not of the account. Other platforms and
the Chinese apps: not confirmed in the research — plan lunchtime (12–13) and evening (20–22)
slots in the audience's time zone and measure. Series content posts at the same slot every
time; the second post of a series goes out within 24 h of the first while the hook is fresh.

## Cover and thumbnail design

- **Face + ≤ 4 words** (≤ 8 CJK chars): the face at 40–60 % of the frame height, looking at the
  camera or at the text; the words in one colour band, one weight.
- **High contrast**: light text on a darkened lower third or dark text on a light band; check
  it at 120 px wide (the 小红书 / 快手 tile is half a phone).
- **One template per series**: same title position, same colour, same type; the episode number
  changes, nothing else. Recognition beats novelty in a grid.
- **Ratio per platform** (ratio-map.md): 16:9 1280×720 YouTube; 3:4 小红书 / IG grid; 9:16 Reels
  / TikTok / 抖音 with the text in the centre 60 % because the grid crops to a taller tile;
  1146×717 B站 with the bottom-left clear; 6:7 视频号 composed for its 1:1 share crop.
- Covers are generated **with** the text only at the final ratio (nano-banana-pro, gpt-image-2.5
  or seedream-5-pro for reliable multilingual type); every other size gets the text overlaid
  after the crop.

## Carousel / 图文 storytelling

1. **Cover as hook** — the question, the contrast or the result; it must work alone in the feed.
2. **One idea per card**, one line of text per card, the same layout grid on every card.
3. **Escalate**: cards 2–N answer the cover in order of surprise, not of logic; the strongest
   proof sits two cards before the end.
4. **Last card = CTA** (follow, save, 主页看全集, 领取), in the same template as the cover so the
   loop back to card 1 feels designed.
5. **One ratio for the whole set** (IG ≤ 20 slides, 小红书 ≤ 18 images, TikTok photo mode ≤ 35):
   generate every card at the same `ratio` and crop to the same box.
6. Caption repeats the cover's keyword in the first line; on 小红书 the 话题 tags close the body.

## Trending audio

Trending sounds lift reach on TikTok / 抖音 / Reels, but a business account may only use
tracks from the platform's **commercially licensed library**; personal-account sounds are
muted or the post is removed when the account is business-typed. AI-generated soundtracks
(Seed Audio, generated per teaser) avoid the problem and can be reused across the series.
Never lip-sync a drama clip to a licensed pop song for a paid promotion.

## What to avoid

- A logo intro, a fade-in, a title card before the hook.
- Letterboxed 16:9 inside a 9:16 post (regenerate — ratio-map.md).
- Text in the top 270 px or the bottom 670 px of a vertical frame; text under the right rail.
- More than 5 hashtags on Instagram; hashtag walls anywhere; hashtags that do not exist yet.
- Sub-1080-px sources, upscaled 1K frames, JPEG re-compression of a re-compression.
- Cross-platform reposts with the other platform's watermark or overlay language.
- Engagement bait ("like if…", fake polls), fake countdowns, fake UI buttons drawn into the frame.
- Claiming AI footage is real footage; skipping the AI label where it is required.
