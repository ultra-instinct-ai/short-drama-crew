# Platform specs — China (抖音, 快手, 小红书, 视频号, B站, 微博, 微信公众号)

The same table shape as platforms-global.md for the mainland platforms: per surface, the ratio
and pixel size, duration and file caps, the safe zone, the text limits, and the conventions that
decide whether a post is read at all. Chinese trade terms are kept in Chinese (图文, 封面, 话题,
标题) because that is how the platform's own upload screens name them. Rows marked *(verify)*
had conflicting figures in the 2025–2026 research; "not confirmed" means no figure was found —
say so in the plan rather than guess. Generation ratios and crop boxes per row are in
ratio-map.md.

## China-specific practice

- **「AI生成」 label.** 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》 has been in force since 2025-09-01: AI-generated
  or AI-edited video, images and audio carry a visible 「AI生成」 label (most platforms add a
  toggle on upload — switch it on) and the file keeps implicit metadata / watermark marking. Do
  not strip the marking in the crop step; do not describe AI footage as real footage.
- **Title style.** 小红书 标题 ≤20 chars and the platform hides more; 抖音 shows ≈20. Front-load the
  keyword the search box will match (product, place, problem), then the twist. Number + result
  patterns (「3个…」「第2集…」) and bracketed qualifiers (【】) read as native; clickbait without the
  payoff in the body is down-ranked.
- **Emoji and keyword conventions.** Emoji as bullet markers at line starts, not inside
  sentences; the body repeats the title's keyword once in the first line; 话题 tags (#…#) go at
  the end of the body, 3–6 on 小红书, and they are searchable — use the platform's suggested
  spellings, not invented ones.
- **图文 storytelling.** The cover is the hook (question, contrast, result), each following image
  is one idea with one line of text on it, the last image is the CTA (follow / 主页看全集 / 领取).
  One ratio for the whole set, one title template for the whole account.
- **Cover text is the headline.** On 小红书 and 快手 the tile is half the screen: ≤8 chars, one
  colour band behind the text, face or product filling the rest.
- **Search first.** 小红书 and 抖音 surface posts by search as much as by feed: the title, the
  cover text and the first body line should agree on one keyword.
- **Platform video-style borrowing.** 抖音 audiences reject an obvious repost from TikTok (English
  overlays, foreign sounds); regenerate the overlay layer in Chinese rather than translating on
  top of the old one.
